This is the first post on this site. I think it wouldn't be appropriate to write about the site itself, so here we are.

I'd be lying if I claimed that anything up to this point ,excluding this first post :), is human-made. After all, most of my current work involves in some way working with AI and specifically claude.

A couple of months ago, I decided it't time for me become a part of the AI community and start working on neither uni coursework nor work-related projects. And it all began with finally getting the claude pro subscription (from work!).

I was immediately both amazed and terrified, I could suddently work on more than 10 projects at once, I could see raw ideas turn into something that works in just a few hours; I had literally 10 terminal windows open at the same time, all running claude in different directories.

Around a month into workflow, the amount of work done was more than i could ever imagine. Projects started getting bigger, more ambitious and increased in numbers. Every problem encountered would generate a new idea that would in turn turn into a completely new project of it own.

This, however, inevitebly aroused some issues. Despite the volume of the total work getting done, I was nowhere near finishing or deploying any one of them. After a while, every project would end up using like an unorganised mess of code, old plan files and ideas that was impossible to navigate. I realised I had nothing to show despite months of continuous work.

This site is a step into trying to deploy something complete as well as having a way to showcase not only finished products but also ideas, works-in-progress projects. It is also a tool that will allow me to write down some thoughts or problems I will be encountering in this journey of exploring AI. 

I'm not sure how long it has been since I last wrote something like this without making use of an llm and to be honest it does feel kind of refreshing. This site was built using AI. Its content will be written by me. (Until I have enough training data for a LORA xD).
